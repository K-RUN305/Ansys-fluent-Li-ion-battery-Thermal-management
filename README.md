# Ansys-fluent-Li-ion-battery-Thermal-management
CFD-based thermal management and cooling optimization of a lithium-ion battery module using ANSYS Fluent, focusing on heat generation, temperature uniformity, coolant flow, and pressure drop.
Developing a 3D CFD model of a lithium-ion battery module to investigate heat generation, coolant flow, temperature distribution, and thermal uniformity under different operating conditions.
Performing parametric analysis of C-rate, coolant flow rate, inlet temperature, and cooling-channel geometry to minimize maximum cell temperature and temperature non-uniformity.
Evaluating pressure drop, heat removal rate, and pumping power to identify an optimized cooling configuration.
